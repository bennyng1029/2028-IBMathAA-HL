The topics

Five strands across the full IB Math AA syllabus. Work through each strand in order.

Topic 1Number and algebra

Patterns, growth, counting, complex numbers, and formal mathematical proof.

  • 1.1 Sequences, series and financial models
  • 1.2 Exponents, logarithms and the binomial theorem
  • 1.3 Counting, partial fractions and extended binomials
  • 1.4 Complex numbers and De Moivre's theorem
  • 1.5 Mathematical proof and systems of linear equations

Topic 2Functions

Mappings, graphs, transformations, and algebraic models that connect variables.

  • 2.1 Lines, functions, domains, inverses and composites
  • 2.2 Quadratics, rational, exponential and log models
  • 2.3 Transformations, polynomials and modulus functions

Topic 3Geometry and trigonometry

Triangles, radians, periodic identities, and vectors, lines, and planes in 3D space.

  • 3.1 3D geometry, triangle trigonometry and radians
  • 3.2 The unit circle, trig identities and periodic models
  • 3.3 Vectors, lines and planes in 3D space

Topic 4Statistics and probability

Summarizing data, correlation, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, and distributions.

  • 4.1 Descriptive statistics, correlation and regression
  • 4.2 Probability, distributions, Bayes and continuous RVs

Topic 5Calculus

Rates of change, optimization, integration, differential equations, and Maclaurin series.

  • 5.1 Differentiation, curve analysis and optimization
  • 5.2 Integration, areas between curves and kinematics
  • 5.3 First principles, L'Hôpital, implicit, parts and volumes
  • 5.4 Differential equations and Maclaurin series

  3. Watch

    Open a worked example. Try the next step on paper before revealing it and checking the markscheme tags.

  4. Try

    Do the “Try it” calculation. Enter a 3 s.f. decimal, a fraction, or an exact value such as pi/3.

Two labels to know

SL and Additional HL

Everything unmarked is Standard Level (SL) core, which both SL and HL students take. Content only Higher Level (HL) students need is marked like this:

This block is Additional HL. It always has a violet edge and badge. If you are revising SL core only, use the switch in the header to hide every block and topic tile like this one.

Not needed for IB

Textbooks and AI tools often bring in university theorems or A-Level techniques that are not in the IB Math AA guide. When that happens, we tell you explicitly so you do not waste study time.

A box like this marks the edge of the IB syllabus. Read it once for peace of mind, then leave it. Nothing inside it needs to be learned for your exam. If it ever appears, the question will give it to you or guide you through it.

Exam facts (May 2028)

The May 2028 examination follows the IB Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches guide (First assessment 2021, final assessment November 2028). You receive a clean copy of the official Formula Booklet in every paper.

Unless a question specifies otherwise, give non-exact numerical answers to 3 significant figures (or exact values on Paper 1). In calculus and circular measure, always set your calculator to radians unless degrees are explicitly stated. For a “Show that” question, never substitute the given final result into your working—derive it step by step.

At HL, the syllabus allocates 240 teaching hours: 39 h for Topic 1, 32 h for Topic 2, 51 h for Topic 3, 33 h for Topic 4, 55 h for Topic 5, and 30 h for the toolkit and the mathematical exploration. At SL, the 150 hours are split as 19, 21, 25, 27, 28, and 30 hours.
