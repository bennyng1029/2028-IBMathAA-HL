## t3-1 — 3D geometry, triangle trigonometry and radians

### Major

1. **Syllabus mislabel: the ambiguous case is SL 3.5, not SL 3.2.**
   - `topics/t3-1.html:219`: `data-kicker="Worked example 2 (SL 3.2 — Ambiguous Case)"`
   - `data/questions-t3-1.js:217` (q012): `syllabus: 'SL 3.2'`
   - In the 2019 AA guide, SL 3.2 covers right-angled trig, the sine rule, the cosine rule and ½ab sin C. "Extension of the sine rule to the ambiguous case" is listed under **SL 3.5** (alongside the unit circle). Note that `docs/research/syllabus-topics-3-4-5.md:25` repeats the same error.
   - Fix: change to `SL 3.5 — Ambiguous Case` and `syllabus: 'SL 3.5'`. Optionally add a note that it is examined with SL 3.2 triangle work.

2. **Decoder SSA row labels the wrong angle and the wrong side** — `topics/t3-1.html:74-75`
   - Quoted: "Given angle `A`, adjacent side `b`, and opposite side `a < b`: the two opposite angles are `C_1 = arcsin((b sin A)/a)` (acute) and `C_2 = 180° - C_1`" and "Fast alternative for the two lengths of `BC = c` … `a^2 = b^2 + c^2 - 2bc cos A`, and solve the quadratic in `c`".
   - What is wrong: with A, b and a given, the sine rule gives sin **B** = b sin A / a, so the ambiguous angle is B (the angle opposite b), not C. Also, in standard labelling BC = a, which is a *given* side. The unknown side found from the quadratic is c = **AB**. A student who follows this row gets both labels wrong. It also disagrees with the widget caption (line 216), which uses B₁ and B₂.
   - Fix: "…the two possible angles are `B_1 = arcsin((b sin A)/a)` (acute) and `B_2 = 180° - B_1` (obtuse)" and "…the two possible lengths of `AB = c`: … solve the quadratic in `c`". In the row header (line 73) also change "two possible values of `BC`" to "two possible values of `AB`" (or "of the third side").

### Minor

3. **Wrong booklet location for the solid formulas** — `topics/t3-1.html:112` ("The Prior Learning page of your IB Formula Booklet gives the volume and curved surface area formulas for right pyramids, right cones, and spheres") and line 116 (caption "3D solids in the IB Formula Booklet (Prior Learning, p. 2)"). Prior Learning only has 2D areas, the cuboid, cylinder and prism, and the 2D distance and midpoint formulas. The pyramid, cone and sphere formulas (and the 3D distance and midpoint formulas) are printed under **SL 3.1**. Fix: "The SL 3.1 section of your Formula Booklet gives…" and caption "(SL 3.1 section)".

4. **Bearings range** — `topics/t3-1.html:42` "(`000°` to `360°` clockwise from North)" and line 252 dfn "from 000° to 360°". Three-figure bearings run from 000° up to, but not including, 360°: 360° is written 000°. Fix: "000° to 359°" or "0° ≤ bearing < 360°".

5. **Confusing table cell** — `topics/t3-1.html:141`: "Sphere: `A = 4pi r^3`? No—`4pi r^2`!". Putting a wrong formula first in a reference table invites misreading. Fix: "Sphere: `A = 4pi r^2` (not r³)".

6. **Garbled condition in the Vieta callout** — `topics/t3-1.html:247`: "and when the two sides `a` form an isosceles triangle at the base, `b_1 - b_2 = 2a cos C_1`!". The identity is correct (2·7·cos 55.02° = 8.03, matching WE2). However, the two positions of side a *always* form an isosceles triangle, so "when" wrongly suggests a special condition. Fix: "and because the two positions of side `a` form an isosceles triangle with base `b_1 - b_2`, `b_1 - b_2 = 2a cos C_1` (C₁ the acute angle)". Note also that this callout calls the adjacent side `c`, while the decoder (line 74) calls it `b`.

7. **Wording** — `topics/t3-1.html:182`: "you must drop a perpendicular from `V` and `M` to the midpoint `N` of side `AB`". Only MN is perpendicular to AB in the base; V is *joined* to N. Fix: "join `V` and `M` to the midpoint `N` of `AB` (both VN and MN are perpendicular to AB) and find `angle VNM`".

8. **Scope (SL 3.1)** — `topics/t3-1.html:39`: "(or between two planes)". SL 3.1 lists only "the angle between two intersecting lines or between a line and a plane". Angles between planes are AHL 3.18 (vector method). This is not harmful as enrichment, but should be flagged. Fix: drop "(or between two planes)", or mark it as extension.

9. **Self-contradictory stem** — `data/questions-t3-1.js:309` (q017): "A **solid** right circular cone … is made by rolling up a semicircular sheet of thin metal…" and "(b) … its circular base". A rolled sheet makes a hollow cone with no base. Fix: "A hollow right circular cone (open at the base)…" and "(b) … the plane of its base".

10. **Stored answer slightly off** — `data/questions-t3-1.js:151` (q008): `answer: 116.847`. The true value is 180° − arcsin(12 sin 42°/9) = **116.852°**. This is within tolerance, so no marking impact. Fix: `answer: 116.852`. The solution's "(116.8°)" is fine. Also, the solution writes C₁ = 63.15°, which gives 116.85°.

## t3-2 — Unit circle, trig identities and periodic models

### Major

1. **Syllabus mislabel: inverse trig functions are AHL 3.9, not AHL 3.11.** The 2019 guide puts "the inverse functions arcsin x, arccos x, arctan x; their domains and ranges; their graphs" under **AHL 3.9**, with reciprocal ratios. AHL 3.10 is compound-angle identities and tan 2θ. AHL 3.11 is "relationships between trigonometric functions and the symmetry properties of their graphs". Affected places:
   - `topics/t3-2.html:274`: "Principal domains and ranges of inverse trigonometric functions (AHL 3.11)" → "(AHL 3.9)"
   - `topics/t3-2.html:319`: `data-kicker="Worked example 3 (AHL 3.10–3.11)"` → "(AHL 3.9–3.10)"
   - `data/questions-t3-2.js:94` (q005): `syllabus: 'AHL 3.11'` → `'AHL 3.9'`
   - `data/questions-t3-2.js:231` (q012): `syllabus: 'AHL 3.11'` → `'AHL 3.9'`
   - (Source of the error: `docs/research/syllabus-topics-3-4-5.md:76,102`.)

2. **Correct exact answers are rejected as "wrong significant figures."** I ran these through `S1.math.checkNumeric`:
   - `topics/t3-2.html:188`: `data-answer="-0.5" … data-sigfigs="2"`. Typing the exact answer `-0.5` returns status `sigfig` ("Give your answer to 2 significant figures").
   - `topics/t3-2.html:345`: `data-answer="0.8" … data-sigfigs="2"`. Typing `0.8` returns `sigfig`.
   - `data/questions-t3-2.js:119-121` (q006): `answer: 1.5625, tolerance: { abs: 0.005 }, sigfigs: 4`. The stem asks for "the exact value … as a decimal". Typing the exact `1.5625` (5 s.f.) returns `sigfig`, as does `1.56`. Only `1.563` is accepted, and that is not exact.
   - Fix: use `data-sigfigs="1-2"` for the two Try-its, and `sigfigs: '3-5'` for q006 (or drop sigfigs, since the answers are exact).

3. **Misconception note attached to the wrong distractor** — `data/questions-t3-2.js:104` (q005, option index 1 = "0"): "arccos(−1/2) is NOT −π/3! …". Taking arccos(−1/2) = −π/3 gives −π/3 + (−π/6) = **−π/2**, which is option 3, not 0. So a student who picks "0" is told about an error they did not make. Fix: move this text to key 3. For key 1, use e.g. "You may have taken arccos(−1/2) = π/6 or arcsin(−1/2) = −2π/3; the principal values are 2π/3 and −π/6." For key 3, use "arccos(−1/2) is NOT −π/3: its range is [0, π]…".

### Minor

4. **Marks mismatch** — `data/questions-t3-2.js:328` (q017): `marks: 6`, but the markscheme lists M1, A1, M1, M1, A1 = **5** marks. Fix: add a mark (e.g. a final AG or R1 line) or set `marks: 5`.

5. **Broken grammar in stem** — `data/questions-t3-2.js:209` (q011): "Solve the equation `2 sec^2 x - 3 tan x - 4 = 0` leads to which quadratic equation in `tan x`?" Fix: "Solving the equation … leads to which quadratic equation in `tan x`?"

6. **Misconception text does not match the distractor** — `data/questions-t3-2.js:316` (q016, option `(sqrt(3) + 1)/2`): "You forgot the denominator factor of 2 in √2/2." (√3+1)/2 comes from dropping the √2/2 factors entirely (treating cos 45° = sin 45° = 1), not from dropping the 2. Fix: "You treated cos 45° and sin 45° as 1 — each is √2/2, so the result is (√2/2)(√3/2 + 1/2) = (√6 + √2)/4."

7. **AHL decoder row not hidden in SL-only mode** — `topics/t3-2.html:80`: `<tr>` for "**[AHL]** “Simplify `cos(arcsin x)` …”" has no `data-level="ahl"`. The CSS rule `:root[data-show="sl"] [data-level="ahl"]` would hide it if it did. Fix: `<tr data-level="ahl">`.

8. **"Show that" results not marked AG** — `topics/t3-2.html:335` (WE3, final line ends `[R1]`), `data/questions-t3-2.js:279` (q014) and `:336` (q017). These are "show that / prove" results. IB markschemes end the given result with **AG** (answer given, no extra mark). Fix: append "AG" to the final line of each.

9. **Paper 1 question asking for 3 s.f.** — `data/questions-t3-2.js:292` (q015): "[AHL · Paper 1] … Give your answer to 3 significant figures (or exact radical form)." Without a GDC the natural answer is (−3+√17)/4. Fix: "Give your answer in the form (a + √b)/c". The numeric checker already accepts exact input.

## t3-3 — Vectors, lines and planes in 3D (AHL)

### Major

1. **Stem gives away the answer, and "exact" contradicts "3 s.f."** — `data/questions-t3-3.js:352` (q015): "Find the exact distance … Give your answer to 3 s.f. or as `3*sqrt(2)`." The answer *is* 3√2 (the point is (3, 3, 0)), so the stem reveals it. Fix: "Find the exact distance from the origin `O` to the point where `L` crosses the `xy`-plane."

2. **False claim in misconception note** — `data/questions-t3-3.js:240` (q010, option √19): "`sqrt(19)` is the distance from `P` to the starting point `A(1, 0, 1)`". With P(1, 3, −2), PA = (0, −3, 3) and |PA| = √18 = 3√2 ≈ 4.243, not √19. Fix: either change the distractor to `3*sqrt(2)` (keeping the note), or rewrite the note: "√19 does not come from PF·b = 0; check your foot-of-perpendicular working."

### Minor

3. **Worked example title is wrong** — `topics/t3-3.html:112`: "Using |u − v|² = (u − v) · (u − v) with unit vectors". In the example |u| = 3 and |v| = 4, so these are not unit vectors. Fix: "…given magnitudes".

4. **Incorrect statement** — `topics/t3-3.html:228`: "A plane `Pi` is uniquely defined by its normal vector `n = (a, b, c)`". A normal vector only fixes the orientation; infinitely many parallel planes share it. Fix: "A plane is uniquely defined by a point on it and its normal vector…".

5. **Odd paper label** — `data/questions-t3-3.js:416` (q018): `paper: 'P3'` on a routine "show skew / shortest distance" question. Paper 3 is the extended problem-solving paper. Fix: `paper: 'P1'` (or `'P2'`).

6. **Widget label typo and inconsistency** — `assets/js/widgets-t3-3.js:26,30`: `t('uᵧ')` and `t('vᵧ')` use U+1D67 GREEK SUBSCRIPT SMALL LETTER GAMMA (renders as a subscript γ, not y). Lines 27 and 31 use `t('u_z')` and `t('v_z')` (literal underscore), next to `uₓ` and `vₓ`. Fix: use consistent labels, e.g. `'u (x)'`, `'u (y)'`, `'u (z)'` (no Unicode subscript y exists), or `x-component of u`, etc.

7. **Figure caption does not match widget** — `topics/t3-3.html:240`: "…gives the angle 90° − ϕ with the vertical normal **n**". The widget has a "Plane tilt" slider (`widgets-t3-3.js:127`), so n is not always vertical. Fix: "…with the normal **n**".
