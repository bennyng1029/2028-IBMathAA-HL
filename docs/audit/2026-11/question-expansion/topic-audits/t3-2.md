How each kind of question was checked:

Asymptote crossings (q027, q028, q039, q051, q052) were filtered out. The full solution sets match the stored answers and solutions for q004, q008, q013, q019, q022, q023, q027, q028, q029, q031, q033, q036, q037, q045, q047, q048, q051, q052, q055, q056 and q059. Every numeric item that asks for one solution names it clearly ("largest", "first", "in π/2 < x < π", or one solution).
- **Sinusoidal models:** amplitude, axis, period, phase and the requested values were recomputed from the stem's data for q007, q009, q020, q021, q024, q025, q026, q032, q034 and q038. This includes the q025 integer day count of 107 (days 119–225), the q026 clock times 10:06 and 7 h 48 min, and the q038 shift of 1 h 40 min = 5/3 h.
- **Short items:** every markscheme line count equals `marks`, and the [n] part marks add up to `marks`.
