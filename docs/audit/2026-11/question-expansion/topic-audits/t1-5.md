## What was checked
- **Linear systems:** every system was solved exactly with sympy (rationals). For each parameter item (q010, q012, q013, q018, q034, q042, q043, q056, q059), the determinant roots were found and the rank of the coefficient matrix and the augmented matrix was used to classify unique / none / infinite at every value the stem asks about, plus control values. Parametric solutions were checked symbolically, and the geometry (q034 prism, no parallel normals) with cross products. The rank-2 balance equations (q041) and the redundant tin equation (q045) were checked.
- **Proofs:** induction items (q017, q024, q025, q026, q027, q052, q057, q058) all have a correct base case, a hypothesis stated "for some k", a step that really uses the hypothesis (checked symbolically), and a conclusion. Contradiction items (q009, q016, q029, q030, q032): the negations are correct and the contradictions are real. Direct proofs (q003, q004, q021, q023): the identities were checked symbolically.
- **Numerical tests:** every claimed formula was tested for many n: Σr·r! (n ≤ 29), 7ⁿ+2 (n ≤ 59), Σr2^(r−1) (n ≤ 59), 11ⁿ−4ⁿ (n ≤ 79), n³−n (|n| < 200), the derivative formulas (n ≤ 9 to 11), Σr/2^r (n ≤ 39), the closed forms of the recurrences (q028, q053, q058), and S(n) for q057 (n ≤ 299). The thresholds were checked by search to 200 or 500 (q014, q027, q051).
- **Marks:** for every short item, marks equal the number of mark lines. Each AG line earns no mark. Every challenge item's first hint starts with "Decode:". Every AHL item is level 'ahl'.
