## New bug found (not in the audit): backslashes lost in the JS banks
In single-quoted JS strings, `'\in \mathbb{R}'` evaluates to "in mathbb{R}", and `'\nmid'` evaluates to a NEWLINE followed by "mid". So 35 stems, options and solutions in
questions-t1-4.js / questions-t1-5.js rendered as "x in mathbb{R}" (t1-5-q016's correct option read "4 mid 2"), and the
translations had copied the broken text.
- **Fixed**: escaped the backslashes in the source (`\\in \\mathbb{Z}^+`, `\\circ`, `\\nmid`), so the central formatter now gets
  `\in` etc. and renders ∈ ℤ⁺, °, ∤. The 6 q.js overlays were repaired the same way. The coordinator suggested Unicode instead.
  I kept escaped LaTeX because it renders the same (∈, ℝ, ℤ⁺, ∤ were checked through S1.math.formula) and keeps every overlay aligned. I ran a grep over data/questions-*.js and found the bug in no other bank.
