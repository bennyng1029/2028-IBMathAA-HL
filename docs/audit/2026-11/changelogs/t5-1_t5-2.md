## t5-1

- **M1 Fixed: the f/f'/f'' widget labelled points as stationary when they are not.** `assets/js/widgets-t5-1.js:178-212, 250-300`. The loose `|f'| < 0.04` test is gone. Each model now lists its exact stationary and inflexion points, worked out by hand and checked with sympy:
  - cubic: max at -1, non-stationary inflexion at 0.5, min at 2
  - statInf: stationary inflexion at 0, non-stationary inflexion at 2, min at 3
  - quarticTrap: flat minimum at 0
  
  A feature is reported only when x₀ is within half a slider step (0.025) of that point. x₀ is then snapped to the exact point, so f' and f'' read exactly 0.00 (0). Everywhere else the label comes from the signs of f' and f'', which are never zero there. Small non-zero readouts now show 2 s.f. (e.g. 0.00013 (+)) instead of a misleading "0.00" or "(0)". I ran a node harness over every slider position (step 0.05) for all three models. Every position gives the right label: no max or min appears near the statInf point at 0, and the quartic shows "min" only at 0. The existing translated strings are reused, so no i18n change was needed.
